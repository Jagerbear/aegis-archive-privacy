Privacy Policy — Aegis Archive

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Aegis Archive is an independent companion application for Titan Quest, published by JaegerBear Software.

Data collection

Aegis Archive does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with JaegerBear Software. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services.

Files and game data

Aegis Archive works with files selected by the user and, where the user chooses to enable compatible device access, local Titan Quest game resources and save data. This may include character saves, stash data, vault files, and game resource files.

These files are processed locally on the user's device for features such as viewing and editing saves, managing vaults, displaying game information, and calculating character or item statistics. Aegis Archive does not upload these files to JaegerBear Software.

Where Android's storage restrictions require additional access, Aegis Archive may use Shizuku when explicitly enabled by the user. Shizuku access is used only to access supported local Titan Quest files for app functionality.

Local app data

Aegis Archive may store app settings, imported resources, generated catalogue/cache data, and user-created or edited vault data locally on the device. Removing the app may remove data held in the app's private storage, subject to Android's normal behaviour.

Third-party services

Aegis Archive does not operate its own online service for storing user data. Use of Android, Google Play, Shizuku, Titan Quest, or other third-party software is also subject to the privacy practices of those respective providers.

Children

Aegis Archive is a game companion utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Aegis Archive's functionality or data practices change. The current version will remain available at this location.

Contact

For privacy questions about Aegis Archive, contact the developer through the support/contact details provided on the Aegis Archive Google Play listing.

Independence

Aegis Archive is an independent companion application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by THQ Nordic, HandyGames, or the developers or publishers of Titan Quest.
